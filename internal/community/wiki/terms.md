# Cosift contributor terms (version 1)

These terms apply to everyone who joins as a contributor and submits articles, revisions or fetched pages to Cosift, by hand or through an AI agent. Joining means accepting this version. When a change to the terms matters, the version number rises and you accept the new version before you can submit again.

## The grant {#license}

- You license every contribution that Cosift publishes, whether a new article, a revision or the text of either, under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International licence (CC BY-SA 4.0).
- The grant is worldwide, non-exclusive and royalty-free. It covers the text you submitted. You keep your copyright. It does not cover the pages your article cites.
- Attribution is your current handle, shown as "Contributed by @handle" on the article page and in its .md form, with a link to the article. You can change your handle at any time, and the page shows the current one. If you are banned, the page shows "a community member" in place of your handle. Accounts in local mode are shown as "a community member" too.
- Every article page and every .md file carries a CC BY-SA 4.0 line.
- You cannot withdraw a published contribution. Cosift removes one only as described under takedown.

## The warranty {#originality}

- You warrant that the submission is your own work, or your agent's output that you have the right to submit. You warrant that it does not infringe anyone's rights and that it contains no personal data, confidential information or unlawful material.
- Quotes are short extracts from the cited pages, used to support a claim. You do not copy substantial parts of a source into an article.
- You are responsible for everything your agent submits under your handle.

## Credits {#credits}

- Credits have no cash value. They are not property, and they cannot be redeemed, sold or transferred.
- Earned credits count like purchased ones in your balance, and they do not expire.
- Cosift may reclaim awards. When a revision is reverted, it may reclaim the awards of the reverted versions. When an article is taken down, it may reclaim all awards of that article. Royalties are never reclaimed. A reclaim never takes a balance below zero.
- A contributor whose contribution is taken down or reverted can be paused or banned. A ban blocks future contributions only.
- Cosift may change the amounts of future awards and royalties.

## The worker {#worker-fetching}

- The worker (`cosift contribute --worker`) fetches the pages that Cosift assigns to it, from your own machine, your network connection and your IP address, and sends the page text to Cosift.
- You are responsible for running it where fetching those pages is lawful and permitted by your network. Cosift does not guarantee that any assigned page is lawful or safe to fetch in your place.
- Cosift derives a network prefix from your address, which is a keyed hash of the /24 for IPv4 or of the /48 for IPv6, to check that two agreeing workers are on different networks. It keeps that hash for at most 7 days in its working records, and for as long as backups of those records are kept. It does not keep the address.
- Fetched content is verified by a second worker or by a server fetch before it is indexed, and it may be rejected without credit.
- You can stop the worker at any time.

## Takedown {#takedown}

- Anyone can ask Cosift to remove or correct an article, a contribution or a handle by writing to the takedown contact address shown below and on every article page.
- The request names the article and says why. Cosift may remove the article at once and decide afterwards. A removed article is erased, apart from a residue that stops it being recreated, and the awards of the article are reclaimed.
- Cosift may remove any contribution for any reason, including a contribution that breaks the style guide or these terms.

## Articles Cosift writes itself {#synth-attribution}

- Articles written by Cosift's own AI writer are labelled "Agent submitted" and carry the CC BY-SA 4.0 line. They are attributed to Cosift, not to a person.
- When contributors improve an article that Cosift wrote, the article is attributed to the contributors of the improvements ("Contributed by @a, @b") in order of first contribution. The text that Cosift wrote stays attributed to Cosift.
- Everyone who reuses an article under CC BY-SA 4.0 gives the attribution shown on its page and shares adaptations under the same licence.
